        Fly the drone: click the screen to capture the mouse, then — WASD move · Q/E down/up · Shift boost (3× on top of sim speed) · Ctrl precision (÷3 for fine positioning, Hovercraft only) · Space hover toggle · ↓ down view (toggle back to front) · ← left view · → right view · I identify mode · U units (metric ↔ aeronautical kt/ft/NM) · M map-primary view · +/- map zoom · V third-person chase cam (aircraft model swaps with sim-speed preset: Mavic 3 / Cessna / Learjet / 777 / UFO) · P pause flight · K toggle Easy Mode (Hovercraft) ↔ Realistic · J toggle altitude tape · H toggle horizon · 1–5 switch aircraft (1 Mavic 3, 2 Cessna 172, 3 Learjet, 4 B777, 5 UFO) · Airplane mode (Cessna / Learjet / 777): A/D bank (ailerons), W/S throttle (no stop / no reverse below stall+10%), Q/E pitch · speed presets in HUD (1× = 50 km/h … 100× = 10 000 km/h) · Airspace tour in the panel (5 min express or full country — orbits each stop) · mouse to look.
