  with high confidence: bird-cage airspace rendering, OSM tile mushiness at
  horizon, static Mavic props. Listed in `.scratch/.../baseline_phase0.md`.

  ratio is 1. Phase 5 dynamic-DPR work won't be measurable on this machine
