**Learned:**
  - `window.__sim.ui` was a latent dev-infrastructure bug: the
    object literal is evaluated at module-eval time, but `ui` is
    assigned inside `async bootstrap()`, so the literal froze
    `ui` as `undefined`.  Fixed by re-assigning after UI
    construction; harmless for production, but blocks any
    automated HUD probe from reaching the renderer.  Same shape
    bug could exist for `tourGuide` (also async-constructed) —
    not investigated this session.
  - The playbook's worry about "drone pitch sign opposite of AH
    convention" turned out to be averted by consistency: by
    matching Three.js mesh.rotation.x = bodyPitch and choosing
    W → negative pitch (nose-down), every consumer (mesh, AH,
    third-person camera) gets the same physical reality from one
    sign.  The lesson: pick ONE sign convention (here, +pitch =
    nose-up, Three.js YXZ), apply it at every boundary, and the
    HUD comes out free.
  - The "coasts to stop over ~1.5 s" pass criterion in the
    playbook was loose — with kDrag=0.5 (time constant 2 s), the
    drone decays to 23% of terminal in 1.5 s (6.55 → ~1.5 m/s),
    not to zero.  Visibly decelerating is what matters for the
    UX win; full stop takes ~4 s.  Matches the playbook's intent.
